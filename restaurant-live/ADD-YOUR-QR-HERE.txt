HOW TO ADD YOUR eSewa PAYMENT QR CODE
=====================================

1. Take a photo / screenshot of your eSewa merchant QR code
2. Rename it exactly:   payment-qr
3. Put it in THIS folder (same folder as customer.html)
   - Use .png, .jpg, .jpeg or .webp  →  e.g.  payment-qr.png
4. Done! No restart needed.

Now on the customer page, every unpaid order shows
"💳 Proceed for Payment — रु <amount>" right below the bill.
Tapping it opens the QR full-screen (page behind gets blurred)
with the exact amount to enter in eSewa, plus a
"✅ I have paid" button that notifies the waiter dashboard.
